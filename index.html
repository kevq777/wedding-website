Guest Concierge

Travel & Stay — Planning Your Trip

Everything you need to know for a smooth, relaxed journey into the heart of Accra.

Flights & Arrival

Fly directly into Kotoka International Airport (ACC). Direct and one-stop flights operate regularly from London Heathrow (British Airways), New York, Washington, and European hubs. Please ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.

Hotel Accommodation

A preferred wedding rate block has been reserved at the Labadi Beach Hotel. Simply quote the Kevin & Shannel Wedding when booking your room. Alternative nearby luxury stays include Villa Monticello and Lancaster Accra.

Getting Around Accra

Ride-hailing apps like Uber and Bolt operate smoothly throughout Accra and accept card or cash payments. The hotel concierge can also arrange dedicated chauffeur drivers for day tours and errands.

January in Accra: Sunny & Golden

January brings Ghana's dry, coastal harmattan season with daytime temperatures around 28°C–31°C (82°F–88°F) and gentle tropical sea breezes in the evening. Lightweight, breathable formalwear is ideal.
